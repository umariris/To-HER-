Project: Birthday website for Milandi, called 'sweetnugget' (birthday 4 Nov).
Mood: dreamy, cute, premium, nostalgic. Strictly shades of blue. Pastel liquid glass for the world, vintage blue paper for every word she reads.

COLORS (only these): --sky-mist #EAF4FF, --ice #D6ECFF, --baby-blue #BFDFFF, --powder-cyan #9FE3F5, --periwinkle #A9B8FF, --cornflower #6E9BFF, --ocean #3D6FD8, --navy #1B2A5C (all ink and text), --midnight #0F1B3D (finale sky), --white #FFFFFF. Paper tones (still blue): --paper #E9F0F7, --paper-aged #D3E0EF, --paper-stain #C4D5EA, --envelope #BFD3EA, --envelope-shade #A9C0E0. Backgrounds: soft vertical gradients between sky-mist, baby-blue and periwinkle. Finale: midnight to ocean with white/ice stars.
GLASS (world and UI only): fill rgba(255,255,255,0.35), 1px border rgba(255,255,255,0.7), inner top-left highlight, soft blue shadow. backdrop-filter blur(14px) ONLY on the password card; everything else fakes glass with gradients and transparency.
FONTS: cursive handwriting. Great Vibes for titles, names and big lines. Dancing Script (weight 500) for all long text, min 24px on phone, 28px on desktop, line-height 1.55, ink color navy at 92% opacity. Nunito only for small UI (buttons, counters). Self-host with @fontsource/great-vibes, @fontsource/dancing-script, @fontsource/nunito.
STACK: Vite + React + Framer Motion + GSAP + canvas-confetti. No backend. All text in src/content.js generated from content.md. Keep every word exactly as written.
RESPONSIVE: one site, two designed layouts. Phone (<768px) designed first: full-screen scenes, touch, 48px tap targets, safe-area padding. Desktop (>=768px): wide scenes, hover reactions, cursor sparkle trail, Tuffy's eyes follow the cursor, text column max 720px. Never just shrink the desktop layout.
PERFORMANCE: 60fps on a mid-range Android. Animate only transform and opacity (one exception: clip-path on the small per-word spans inside LiveWriting). Max 3 parallax layers, 30 particles. Unmount the previous scene. No live blur filters and no animated SVG filters (zoom-blur = pre-made layers scaling/fading + a pre-blurred copy crossfading). Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Music only after a tap, mute toggle always visible.

VINTAGE PAPER (CSS only, blue-tinted, used for every card, letter, panel and open-when note): base --paper with aged blotches from radial gradients in --paper-aged and --paper-stain at the corners and edges; one small pre-rendered noise tile (under 20KB, tiled, about 6% opacity) for grain; a rough deckled edge made with a static clip-path or mask (never animated); faint fold creases as thin linear gradients; a soft blue drop shadow. Ink is navy. Never animate filters on paper. Cards also get a small stamped number ('No. 7') in --ocean, a strip of ice-colored washi tape on one corner, and a slight rotation.

POMERANIAN named Tuffy (Pomeranian.jsx, layered inline SVG, blue shades only; she loves Pomeranians; use the name Tuffy in any UI text that mentions the pup): a chibi, very fluffy Pomeranian, head wider than body. Fur in baby-blue fading to ice and white tips, a big scalloped fluffy ruff around the face and neck, small pointed ears with periwinkle insides, a curly fluffy tail curled over its back. BIG round glossy eyes with BLUE irises (cornflower with an ocean-blue ring), dark navy pupils and two white highlights each. Tiny navy nose, small smile with a powder-cyan tongue, soft glowing powder-cyan cheeks, tiny front paws and back feet. Groups: body, head, ruff, earL, earR, eyes, nose, mouth, cheeks, tail, pawL, pawR, footL, footR, cardStack. Moods via Framer Motion variants: idle (breathe 3s, blink every 3-5s, tail wag, ear perk), hop (squash, stretch, landing squash), trip (stumble, tumble, cards toss, tail up), shy (ears down, head tilt, cheeks glow), wave (front paw wave), happy (fast tail wag, bounce, open smile). Eyes follow the cursor (max 3px) on desktop.

SOUNDS (optional files in public/audio; any missing file is skipped silently with no errors): song.mp3 = looping background music at volume 0.35, fades between scenes, starts only after her first tap on the password screen; pen.mp3 = plays only while LiveWriting is writing, volume 0.15; paper.mp3 = envelope flap and opening a card; pop.mp3 = Moments bubbles. One mute toggle controls every sound and remembers her choice (localStorage inside try/catch).

LIVE WRITING (LiveWriting.jsx, reusable; used for the letter pages and the open-when letters): makes handwritten text appear as if someone is writing it in real time. Split the text into words and render every word as one inline-block span from the start (never split single letters, so the cursive joins stay intact, and the page layout never shifts). Unwritten words are hidden with clip-path: inset(0 100% 0 0); each word's reveal animates clip-path to inset(0 0 0 0) from left to right using a CSS animation-delay chain. Timing: about 45ms per letter inside a word, 90ms between words, plus 180ms after a comma, 420ms after a full stop, 800ms between paragraphs. A tiny quill-pen SVG in --ocean follows the writing edge, moved by one element using transform only, tilting slightly, and it rests at the end of the last line when finished. Each word starts in --ocean ink and dries to --navy over 600ms after it finishes. Start when the page scrolls into view (IntersectionObserver), pause when it leaves, never restart once finished (then render plain text and remove the extra spans to free the DOM). Tap anywhere on the page to finish it instantly. A soft pen-scratch sound plays only if public/audio/pen.mp3 exists and the site is unmuted. With prefers-reduced-motion show the text instantly.

SCENES, in order:
1 Intro: 3 layers of drifting SVG clouds on a sky-mist to baby-blue gradient. After 2s a line fades in: 'Something is waiting for you'. After 3s a glass card (real blur) asks 'What do I call you?' with an input and an Open button, text in Great Vibes. Compare after trim, lowercase and removing spaces to 'sweetnugget'. Wrong: card shakes, show the next wrong-answer line (rotate, never the same twice in a row). Correct: success line for 1.5s, start the music if a track exists, then the zoom-blur into a clean bright sky.
2 TuffyCards: Tuffy trots in with a card stack balanced on his back, says the arrival line in a speech bubble on small vintage paper, trips; 19 vintage paper cards scatter in a scrollable field (2-3 screens tall on phone, a wide scatter on desktop), random rotation -8 to 8 degrees, gentle float, only animate visible cards. Unopened cards show their stamped number, title in Great Vibes and a tiny sparkle. Tap: the card expands (shared layout transition) to a full-screen vintage paper sheet with the text in Dancing Script, scrolling inside, over a softly dimmed backdrop. 'Got it' appears after 3s (small progress ring); swipe-down close is disabled until then. On Got it: the card shrinks into Tuffy's basket, counter 'n / 19 collected', a faded outline stays behind, Tuffy reacts with an afterEach line (rotate) and a shy or happy mood. After the 19th: the afterLast line and a button 'Open the letter'.
3 Letter (envelope and letter): a closed envelope of aged blue paper (--envelope body, --envelope-shade flap) centered on a soft blue desk. Front: handwritten address in Great Vibes ('To: Milandi', 'From: Jaipur'), a small blue postage stamp with a Pomeranian face, a postmark 'NOV 4', and a blue wax seal (--ocean) with an embossed heart. Tap the seal: the seal cracks (scale + opacity), the flap opens (3D rotateX, transform only), the folded letter slides up out of the envelope and unfolds in three folds (transform only) until it fills the screen. The letter is a real letter layout on vintage paper: place and date top right ('Jaipur, 4 November 2026'), salutation 'My dearest Milandi,', the body in Dancing Script, the five chapters as five pages (swipe or scroll), each page starting with its title in Great Vibes, ribbon 'Page n of 5' with a scaleX progress bar, then the closing 'With all my love,', the signature 'Yours, always' in Great Vibes, and a P.S. on the last page. The text is written live by the LiveWriting component (see LIVE WRITING), one page at a time, as if a pen is writing it in real time. Tuffy peeks from a corner. Button at the end.
4 YouPage: 6 full-height scroll-snap vintage paper panels, titles in Great Vibes, text in Dancing Script, one slow cloud parallax layer behind. A fixed 'Cheek meter' pill: a bar that fills with scroll (calm, warm, glowing, blushing, off the charts); at 100% Tuffy's eyes sparkle, his tail wags fast and one sparkle burst plays. Button at the end.
5 Moments: 8 floating glass bubbles; tapping one grows it into a small vintage paper note showing one line from the moments array, with a sparkle pop, then it shrinks into a small star left in the sky. After all 8, a continue button. No photos.
6 OpenWhen: 5 vintage blue envelopes with handwritten labels (a column on phone, a row on desktop). Tap: 3D flap-open (rotateX), the letter slides out onto vintage paper in a large sheet in Dancing Script, written live with LiveWriting. Tuffy waves a paw. After all 5, a continue button.
7 Finale: the background turns from sky to midnight, max 40 twinkling stars, a moon in the corner, the finale paragraphs fade in on scroll on a vintage paper sheet, the last line 'I love you, my sweetnugget.' in very large Great Vibes, then one canvas-confetti burst (max 120 pieces, DESIGN colors only). A button 'The making of' opens the MakingOf text on vintage paper. Tapping the moon 5 times shows the easterEgg text in a Tuffy speech bubble.
