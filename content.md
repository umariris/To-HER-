## The Cards (19)

### Card 1: Hi, sweetnugget

Hi, sweetnugget. Yes, you. Happy birthday. 🎂

Before you read anything else, I want you to know that every word on this screen was written for you and only you. There’s a small world here with your name on every wall.

Every card on this screen is something I love about you. Some are funny, some are soft, and some might make you want to hide behind your phone. That’s allowed. Take your time. Read every one. Okay? Pick them up one by one, and Tuffy and I will be waiting at the end.

### Card 2: Your eyes

If I met you at a party, I’d notice your eyes first. I’d probably forget how to speak after that. I love staring at them in your photos, and I’ve decided that in direct sunlight they turn honey-brown, maybe even woody brown. Someone needs to confirm this, and I volunteer.

Here’s my secret wish: I want to stand close enough to find my own reflection in them. Just a tiny me, smiling like an idiot, living in your eyes. I love all of you, but your eyes are my favorite place to look. They’re kind, they’re clever, they’re a little mischievous, and they say things you haven’t said out loud yet. I notice every single one.

And I’ll admit something: sometimes I stare at your photos and look only at your eyes, and I forget everything else in the picture. I love all of you, completely, but your eyes are the part I’d pick out of a crowd of a thousand.

### Card 3: Your laugh

There’s a way a child’s face changes when you hand them their favorite toy. There’s a way a cold ice cream feels on a hot summer afternoon, right when you needed it. That is your laugh to me. And I really, really love ice cream.

I don’t know how to explain what it does to me, except that you can make me blush just by laughing. Through a screen. From the other side of the country. You laugh and I forget what I was saying, and then I lose my train of thought, and then I’m smiling at my phone like I won something. And honestly, I did.

It’s such a small thing, a laugh, but yours has a strange power. Heavy days feel lighter. Tired days feel shorter. And somehow, even when we’re a whole country apart, I hear it and feel like I’m standing right next to you.

### Card 4: The frown

The cutest thing you do, and you have no idea, is that frown. The one that shows up when I’m not taking care of myself. When I push too hard, when I ignore how tired I am, when I say I’m fine and I’m clearly not. You get angry, and it’s the most caring kind of angry in the world.

Most people get annoyed when someone scolds them. I get warm. Because it means somebody noticed. Somebody out there actually worries about me, and I didn’t know how much I needed that until you. So, baby, I’ll try to listen. And I’ll keep secretly loving that frown.

So when you frown at me for ignoring myself, know that it works. It reaches me. Somewhere between Jaipur and Assam, I stop, take a breath, and actually look after myself, because you asked me to, and because you’re worth staying well for.

### Card 5: Our normal day

A normal day with you starts with my good-morning voice note and your morning selfie. It starts with an “I love you” from you before I’ve even looked at my to-do list. Then my day takes over: meetings, college, the family business, my own business. I work, I run around, and I miss you the whole time, in the background, like a song I can’t stop humming.

But the night is the best part. When I’m completely broken down with tiredness, I call you, and your voice does something nothing else can. It gives me strength. I hear you and I think, okay. I can do this. I can do all of it. That’s what a normal day with you is: a small, steady miracle.

Some people wait for big moments to feel loved. I get mine in small, steady pieces: a voice note, a selfie, a tiny “I love you.” Piece by piece, they have become the best part of my life.

### Card 6: The girl behind the stories

Most people know the girl on social media: the one who posts stories, who looks put together, who everyone loves. And she’s real, she’s wonderful. But I know the girl behind her. The one who is still a child in the best ways. The one who gets clumsy, who gets a little crazy, who gets scared, who needs to be held more than she lets on.

That’s the girl I want to hold. I know you’re strong. Stronger than me, honestly. But I’d love the chance to be there, even if all I have is a little strength. If I can take even one thing off your shoulders so you don’t have to carry everything alone, that’s enough for me. That would be enough.

You don’t owe the world the soft version of yourself. But if you ever want to be soft, I’m the place for it. No filters, no stories, no performance. Just you, being you, being held.

### Card 7: Clumsy and mature

I don’t know how to say this properly, but you are the most clumsy and the most mature person I know, at the very same time. One minute you’re tripping over something that wasn’t there, and the next you’re handling a problem with so much calm that I just stop and stare. How does one person hold both?

Your friends would say you’re a little clumsy, a little crazy. I’d say yes, and she’s mine. I wouldn’t change either part. The clumsy one makes me laugh. The mature one makes me trust. Together, they make you you.

Tell me one other person who can trip over their own feet and then calmly fix a problem that has everyone else panicking. I’ll wait. (I already know the answer: nobody.)

### Card 8: The day you stayed calm

There was one day, one really hard one, when everything felt like it was falling apart. I was panicking. I was crying. I didn’t know what to do. And you were calm. You spoke slowly, you thought clearly, you handled it with a maturity that I’m still amazed by.

That’s the day I understood how strong you really are. And I’ll be honest: I still want to give you whatever strength I have, even if you don’t need it. Because you shouldn’t have to be the strong one all the time. Sometimes the strongest person in the room deserves to be taken care of.

You made me feel safe that day, and I don’t think you even realized it. That’s the thing about you: you take care of people without announcing it.

### Card 9: Overthinking

When you have a bad day, you overthink every little bit. I’ve noticed. And here’s what I believe: overthinking isn’t a flaw. It just means you care deeply. The real problem is when the thing you’re overthinking has nothing to do with you, when it was never your fault at all.

So here’s my promise: I’ll be the one who tells you, “Baby, overthink all you want, but this topic isn’t yours to carry.” I’ll say it as many times as it takes. And I’m right here with you, so you never have to untangle it alone.

And on the days when your mind won’t be quiet, just say my name. We’ll sit with it together until the noise passes. Overthinking is no match for two people who refuse to let go of each other, even across a whole country.

### Card 10: Thirty-plus questions

Do you remember our first real conversation? I asked you more than thirty questions. At once. All in one go. And you answered every single one, with a surprised little “why are you asking me thirty questions?” energy. I just wanted to know you. All of you. Your favorites, your fears, your little things.

That was the day I thought: damn, she’s cool. And I was right. You weren’t unapproachable at all. You were friendly, warm, funny, and honest. I found you attractive, yes, but not only because you’re pretty. It was how you talk, how you handle things, how you make a conversation feel like home.

I think about those thirty questions a lot. They were the beginning of everything. One burst of curiosity turned into the most important conversation of my life, and you, in your surprised, wonderful way, answered every single one.

### Card 11: The red light

Here’s a confession. When I’m riding my bike and I get stuck at a red light, I turn off the engine, take out my phone, and message you. Even if it’s only for a minute. Even in the middle of traffic, with everyone honking. That small pause is the most beautiful part of my day.

Between college, the family business, and my own work, my days are full. But I find every gap I can, because each message to you is a reminder that I have someone. I have you. And I love you. In the loudest, busiest places, that’s the quiet I find.

If you ever hear a bike engine go quiet somewhere in Jaipur, that’s me, stopping the whole world for a minute just to talk to you.

### Card 12: “I do love you”

I say “I love you” all the time. You know that. I say it first, I say it often, I say it like I can’t help it. Then one day you said, “I do love you.” Just like that. I was so surprised and so blushing that I think I stopped breathing for a second.

Now every time I send you an “I love you,” your reply plays on a loop in my head. I even keep your pictures in my wallet, in my card holder, so I can look at them when the day gets heavy. And I smile. And then I giggle. Like someone who got really, really lucky.

Hearing you say it back is a feeling I can’t explain. It’s like all my I-love-yous finally got their answer. And my answer to you, every single time, is yes. I choose you.

### Card 13: Your peaceful life

You told me what you dream of: a peaceful, quiet life. Just that. And I believe in that dream with everything I have. You have friends who stand behind you, and a family that, like most Indian families, loves hard and worries louder. But I want you to know you’re not alone in any of it. I’m with you.

And if one day you tell me you need peace, I might just kidnap you and give you that life. (I’m joking. Mostly.) You deserve calm mornings, safe days, and a heart that finally gets to rest.

Whatever your dream looks like, I want to be the one who helps carry it. Not for a season. For every season.

### Card 14: Why “sweetnugget”

You might be wondering where the name came from. Here’s the honest answer: it started with all those questions. The more I learned about you, the sweeter you got, and somewhere in there I just wanted to call you nugget. It doesn’t have a deep meaning. You’re so sweet, it fit, and it stuck.

It’s a name only I get to call you. Nobody else has it. It’s small, silly, and a little ridiculous, and it’s ours. When I type it, I smile. When I say it, I mean it. Sweetnugget. One word, no gaps, all yours.

### Card 15: Mine

There’s a word I love saying about you, and it’s “mine.” Not in a way that holds you down. In the way that says I’m proud of you, I’m lucky to have you, and I’m not going anywhere. When your friends describe you, they’ll say you’re a little clumsy, a little crazy. And I’ll quietly think: mine.

I love saying it in my head, in my messages, in the middle of busy days. It feels like a small secret I get to keep. I hope it feels like a warm blanket to you, never a lock. Mine, as in: I’ve got you. Mine, as in: you’re my person.

### Card 16: Every morning

Mornings are my favorite because of you. A good-morning voice note from me, a selfie from you, and an “I love you” before the world gets loud. I wouldn’t trade that start for anything. It’s ridiculous how much one small selfie can change my mood, how much better the day gets once I’ve seen your face.

Then the day pulls me in a hundred directions, but I carry that morning with me. In meetings, in traffic, in the middle of work, it’s still there, tucked away like a warm little secret. You are how my days begin, and I never want that to change.

### Card 17: Before every call

Do you know what my mind looks like in the minutes before a call with you? Messy. A little loud. Full of things I want to say and no idea where to start. And then you answer, and everything goes quiet except you.

Sometimes I actually lose the topic, because I get so caught up in your voice. It’s addictive. I just focus on it, and I forget what I was supposed to be talking about. If I ever go silent on a call, it’s not because I’m bored. It’s because I’m listening to the sound of my favorite person. You can have my whole attention, always.

### Card 18: Smiling at my phone

I’ve caught myself smiling at my phone more times than I can count. When you type “good morning.” When you send a silly message. When you say “I love you.” I smile, then I giggle, then I blush, and then I’m glad nobody’s watching.

The truth is, I think I’m falling for you all the time. Not once. Not on one special day. Every day, a little more, in tiny pieces: a laugh, a frown, a voice note. You keep giving me new reasons, and I keep falling. I’m not even trying to stop.

### Card 19: Why you matter

Here’s the biggest thing I want to tell you. You’re the person I want to hold even when time gets heavy. Not only on the good days. In every single situation: the easy ones, the hard ones, the boring ones, the ones where we don’t know what to do. I want to stay.

I’ll never leave you alone in any of it. I know I’m not stronger than you, but whatever strength I have is yours, whenever you need it. You matter more to me than I know how to say in a card. That’s why I wrote you a letter too. So keep going, sweetnugget. There’s more waiting for you.

### Chapter 1: Happy birthday, Milandi

Happy birthday, babe. Happy birthday, baby. I know you don’t like to read long messages, and I know this one is way too long. But you told me you’d read it if I wrote it, so here I am, holding you to your word.

Milandi, today the world got a person it doesn’t deserve and somehow received her anyway. I’m so glad you were born. I’m so glad you’re in my life. Take your time with this. Read it slowly, the way you’d taste something sweet.

### Chapter 2: What I wish for your days

I wish that every single day is the best day of your life. I wish that every day you get to know your deeper self a little better, the quiet parts, the brave parts, the parts you haven’t met yet. I wish you find more of the beautiful things I see in you, so that one day you can see them too. Until then, I’ll tell you how beautiful you are, every single day.

Whatever shape your days take, I want you to smile through all of them. Because you are not alone.

And on the days you feel small, I hope you’ll remember this: you were never meant to shrink. You were meant to be the brightest thing in the room, and you already are.

### Chapter 3: What I wish you’d stop doubting

I wish you’d stop doubting your prettiness. I really do. Because it’s not just your face, though your face is incredible. It’s your heart, your honesty, your softness, your strength. I wish you’d stop doubting how good a person you are, because I’ve watched you be that person when nobody was clapping.

You are beautiful from the inside, Milandi. More than you let yourself believe. Every time you doubt it, please remember: I see you clearly, and what I see is wonderful.

Please be as kind to yourself as you are to everyone else. Look in the mirror and try to see what I see: someone I would choose again and again.

### Chapter 4: What I promise

In ten years, I will still say I love you. At thirty, I will still say I love you. At sixty, I will still say it. And at ninety, if I’m alive, I’ll say it again, a little slower and a little louder.

I will tell you you’re beautiful on the days you don’t believe it. I will be the one who tells you the overthinking isn’t yours to carry. And I’ll be there on the heavy days, especially on the heavy days.

That isn’t a poetic promise I’ll forget by morning. It’s a plain, daily one. I’ll keep showing up, and I’ll keep saying it, in words, in voice notes, and at every red light I stop at.

### Chapter 5: One last wish

May this year be gentle with you. May it bring peace you can feel in your bones, people who treat you the way you deserve, and a long list of small happy moments you’ll forget to count. May you laugh more than you worry.

And may you always remember that in Jaipur there’s someone stopping at every red light, just to tell you he loves you. Happy birthday, my sweetnugget.
